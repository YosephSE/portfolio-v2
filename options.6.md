# Option 6 - calm, professional

I'm a software engineer who enjoys building products that sit at the intersection of AI, backend systems, and everyday usability. Most of my work has been about turning messy technical constraints into software that feels reliable, fast, and easy to use.

Most recently, I was the founding engineer at **Alactive**, where I led development of a multi-agent system integrated with WhatsApp, Gmail, Outlook, web clients, and multiple property-management platforms. Some of those integrations came through public APIs, and some required careful reverse engineering. I also built internal tools for monitoring, analyzing, and maintaining the system in production.

Before that, I worked across a mix of backend-heavy and product-driven roles. At **Datafinity**, I worked on three MVPs for startups, helping turn early product ideas into working software. At **GO PLATFORM**, I studied competitor products and reverse-engineered closed systems to better understand their underlying workflows. Earlier, at **Brighter Generation**, I helped build a chronic-care monitoring system used in hospital environments.

Outside of work, I keep building products of my own, including **Tabs**, a cross-browser extension for saving sessions, and an **Application Tracking System** with AI-assisted candidate ranking. I'm also completing a software engineering degree at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University.
