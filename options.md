# Portfolio About Section — Intro Options

Five options for the About section (~200 words, 2–3 paragraphs). Mix of tones; full arc (Alactive, past work, side projects).

## Option 1 — dry, self-aware

I'm an AI engineer who spends most days convincing systems that weren't designed to talk to each other to do exactly that. At Alactive, I'm the founding engineer on a multi-agent platform that lives inside WhatsApp, Gmail, Outlook, web clients, and a handful of property-management systems — some of which have proper APIs, and some of which I had to reverse-engineer politely. A model router I built cut response time by 26%, which is my favorite kind of optimization: the boring kind that actually shows up in production.

Before agents were the whole job, I was deep in backends. At Datafinity I rebuilt a Supabase architecture and dropped query latency 2.1×; at Brighter Generation I led a team of four shipping a chronic-care monitoring system that cut hospital waiting time by 40 minutes. On the side I keep shipping my own stuff — Tabs, a cross-browser extension for hoarding sessions without 80 open tabs, and an ATS with AI-assisted candidate ranking.

TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, Python. Currently finishing my CS degree in Addis Ababa.
